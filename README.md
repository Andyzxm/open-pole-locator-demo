# Open Pole Locator map (view-only)

Utility poles located by Open Pole Locator in the processed areas (Knoxville, Seattle, Apex, Newton, Portland area), with Mapillary's own pole detections and public utility pole records for comparison.

Street-level photos (including the cropped pole photos in data/photos, with the detected box drawn), derived pole points and Mapillary detections: (c) Mapillary contributors, CC BY-SA 4.0; the photo crops are shared under the same license. Utility records: Seattle City Light, Town of Apex and City of Newton public data. Base maps: (c) OpenStreetMap contributors, OpenFreeMap. Page code: Apache 2.0.
