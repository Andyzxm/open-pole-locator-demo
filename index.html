Open Pole Locator

Utility poles found in open street-level photos (Mapillary) and placed on the map, one dot per pole, each with a chance of being a real pole and an error in meters. Draw a box anywhere in the U.S. to find its poles live (about 3 minutes per km², where Mapillary has street photos), or browse the areas already processed.

How sure must a dot be?

45%
Every dot comes with a chance that it is a real utility pole (from a model trained on utility records). The map shows only dots at least this likely. Higher: fewer dots, almost all real poles, some poles missed. Lower: more dots, more poles found, more mistakes (5% shows nearly everything we found). 45% is the tested balance.

Accuracy against utility records (within 10 m)

Precision: share of dots within 10 m of a real pole. Recall: share of the real poles within 30 m of a photo that get a dot. Records that cannot be classified are left out. Precision is a lower bound (some "wrong" dots are real poles missing from the records).
